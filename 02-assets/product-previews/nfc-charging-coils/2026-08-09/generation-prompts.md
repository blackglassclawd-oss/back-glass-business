## Variant A

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: draft ecommerce product image, visual-direction preview only
Primary request: Create an original photorealistic studio product photograph of a smartphone wireless NFC charging coil and attached flex-cable assembly. This is a generic representative part, not a depiction of a specific branded SKU.
Scene/backdrop: pure clean white seamless studio background
Subject: one circular copper induction coil mounted on a thin black backing plate, with an attached matte-black flexible ribbon cable, a small flash-module housing, and fine connector contacts
Style/medium: accurate macro ecommerce product photography
Composition/framing: straight top-down flat-lay, centered, large enough to fill about 75 percent of a square canvas, generous even white padding
Lighting/mood: neutral softbox lighting, crisp but natural metal detail, very light grounding shadow
Materials/textures: visible concentric copper windings, matte polymer backing, realistic flexible circuit ribbon, tiny metallic contacts
Constraints: entirely original image; no logos, trademarks, model numbers, labels, text, barcode, packaging, watermark, hands, tools, phone body, or decorative props; clean silhouette; no cropped cable ends
Avoid: copying any supplier photograph, blue protective-film tabs, plastic retail bag, colored fabric background, exaggerated sci-fi styling, impossible connector geometry
```

## Variant B

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: draft ecommerce product image, alternate visual-direction preview only
Primary request: Create an original photorealistic studio photograph of a complete smartphone wireless NFC charging coil assembly with attached flex cable and flash-module housing. This must read as a high-end B2B repair-parts catalog photograph, while remaining a generic concept rather than a model-specific fitment image.
Scene/backdrop: seamless pure white background
Subject: circular copper induction coil integrated into a thin irregular matte-black backing sheet, with one realistic black flexible ribbon cable extending to a tiny connector and a small pale flash-module lens
Style/medium: sharp professional macro product photography, restrained and technical
Composition/framing: straight top-down flat lay on a portrait 4:5 canvas, subject centered and filling about 60 percent of the frame, complete silhouette visible with generous white margins
Lighting/mood: bright neutral studio light, controlled soft grounding shadow, accurate copper and black material contrast
Materials/textures: fine copper windings, thin layered adhesive backing, matte flexible circuit film, minute gold connector contacts
Constraints: entirely original image; no logos, trademarks, model identifiers, labels, words, numbers, barcode, packaging, watermark, hands, tools, phone body, promotional badges, or decorative props; do not crop any extremity
Avoid: copying any supplier image, plastic bags, blue protective-film tabs, colored fabric, dramatic reflections, floating parts, duplicated connectors, impossible branching cables, sci-fi styling
```
