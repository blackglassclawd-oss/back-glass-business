# iPhone 16 Series Half Assembly / No Coil Review Set

Status: **PREVIEW ONLY - NOT APPROVED FOR SHOPIFY**

This folder contains unbadged catalog-image previews based on Michael's approved
construction reference. The supplier/reference photo was not used as a
storefront image. It was used only to define the exposed internal construction.
Official Apple imagery was used only as a finish-color reference.

## Required construction shown

- Segmented circular magnet ring remains installed.
- Metallic heat-dissipating film fills the center of the ring.
- Microphone bracket and related small preinstalled parts remain installed.
- Charging coil, NFC charging flex, and charging flex cable are not installed.
- Images use the existing Back Glass Pros white-background, overlapping
  exterior/interior presentation.
- The exterior panel and exposed interior end on the same bottom baseline.
- No Premium or A Grade badges are embedded in the images.
- Every large camera aperture is an open hole with the white studio background
  visible through it; no camera module or lens glass is installed.
- Small flash, microphone, and LiDAR/sensor openings retain their distinct
  hardware appearance and are not treated as camera apertures.
- Exterior finishes track Apple's official iPhone 16 and iPhone 16 Pro colors.
