## Strategic Findings

1. **Ordering convenience is table stakes.** MobileSentrix has bulk cart;
   PhoneLCDParts has Quick Order, an app, and reorder claims. Back Glass Pros
   must prove faster, more accurate rear-part ordering rather than merely offer
   another quantity grid.
2. **Broad competitors own scale language.** Back Glass Pros should not promise
   breadth it does not have. Its credible position is specialist configuration
   accuracy and evidence.
3. **Parts4Cells is already targeting repair-shop search intent.** Its recent
   content covers back glass, grades, sourcing, and unit economics. A generic
   blog strategy would enter an existing race with no differentiation.
4. **The content moat is operational evidence.** Grade standards, assembly and
   coil definitions, photographed included components, fitment, QC methodology,
   stock timestamps, defect/RMA trends, and delivered-cost logic are more
   defensible than generic articles.
5. **Back Glass Pros' current weakness is proof, not just metadata.** Search
   Console, analytics, rank, backlink, review, customer-language, grade, and
   service baselines are still missing.
