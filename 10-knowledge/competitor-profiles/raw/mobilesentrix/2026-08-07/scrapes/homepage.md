# MobileSentrix Homepage Raw Notes

**URL:** https://www.mobilesentrix.com/
**Captured:** 2026-08-07
**Method:** Public search/discovery rendering. Direct command-line access returned
HTTP 403 from Cloudflare, so this is not a complete Firecrawl export.

## Captured facts and claims

- The site presents MobileSentrix as a wholesale repair-parts supplier with
  account approval and login for wholesale access.
- The current discovery snapshot advertises more than 18,000 SKUs, repair
  parts, pre-owned devices, accessories, tools, buyback, shipping, marketing
  materials, and API access.
- It displays repair-parts warranty, pre-owned-device warranty, ISO certificate,
  payment, and distributor marks as trust signals.
- It offers country-specific storefronts for the United States, Canada, the
  United Kingdom, and Europe.
- These are competitor-published statements. This research did not independently
  validate SKU count, certificates, warranty coverage, stock, or service level.

## Access note

The Cloudflare challenge is itself relevant to agentic discovery: normal search
systems can surface content, but some direct automated clients receive a 403.
