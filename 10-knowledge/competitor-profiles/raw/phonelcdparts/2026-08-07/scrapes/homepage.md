**Method:** Public search/discovery rendering plus HTTP header check.

## Captured facts and claims

- The homepage displays public prices, product specifications, quantity
  controls, search, account, and cart functions.
- Best-seller cards include explicit assembly, material/grade, refresh-rate,
  transfer-eligibility, warranty, model, and price terms.
- Service links include Quick Order, RMA, buyback, trade-off, Request Part, and
  an app download.
- The footer identifies a Wayne, New Jersey address and US phone number.
- Public App Store copy says the app provides model search, real-time stock,
  order tracking, saved history, repeat ordering, and restock alerts. This audit
  did not test the app or verify the operational claims.

The homepage returned HTTP 200 to a direct request. Headers identify Magento
with Hyva Themes and include Google analytics/tag domains in the content
security policy; this does not prove a particular analytics property is active.
