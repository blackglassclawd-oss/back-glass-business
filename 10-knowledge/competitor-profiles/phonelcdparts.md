cards expose model, assembly, material/grade, technical eligibility, warranty,
and price. Its About page supplies entity and scale claims, while the app and
service navigation address search, repeat ordering, availability, support, and
RMA needs.

- Quick Order and repeat-order features directly overlap the proposed Back

## Implications For Back Glass Pros

PhoneLCDParts proves that Quick Order, an app, real-time-stock language, saved
orders, and broad technical naming already exist in the category. Back Glass
Pros must win through measurable rear-part accuracy and evidence, not a feature
checklist. Its specialist model/assembly/coil taxonomy can be more focused, but
only after catalog completeness and parity are proven.
