# Business

## Identity and market

- Brand: Back Glass Pros.
- Business model: B2B cell-phone repair-parts distribution.
- Primary working audience: mobile repair shops.
- Current specialty: Apple iPhone back glass, rear assemblies, and planned
  charging-flex products.
- Shopify store: `kfczyu-kc.myshopify.com`.
- Intended public domain: `backglasspros.com`.

The working commercial promise is:

> Right model. Right color. Right assembly. Known delivered cost.

This is a positioning draft, not an approved public claim.

## Evidence-backed operating snapshot

- A 2026-07-26 public capture contains 66 products and 312 variants. All 312
  captured variants had unique SKUs and were publicly marked available.
- The latest authenticated export completed on 2026-08-06 and contains 84
  product roots, 384 inventory roots, and five order roots.
- The most recent documented operational audit reports 13 customer records, no
  Shopify B2B companies, and two inventory locations.
- Shopify remains authoritative for catalog management, checkout, payments,
  tax, orders, refunds, and fulfillment during migration.

The public capture and authenticated counts are different populations. Do not
use one as a substitute for the other.

## Governance

- Michael is the product and catalog authority.
- Jason owns software and infrastructure architecture decisions.
- Market, competitor, and other research agents may analyze data and recommend
  changes across business categories.
- Agents do not approve their own recommendations. Jason may approve a change
  when the supporting evidence is compelling and the expected commercial
  result is measurable.
- Buzz is intended to increase business efficiency through evidence, tracked
  decisions, explicit approval, and measurable outcomes.

The final rule for resolving a disagreement between product judgment and a
data-backed commercial recommendation is still open.

## Success hierarchy

1. Correct-part confidence.
2. Defined and evidenced quality.
3. Current stock and replenishment confidence.
4. Known delivered cost.
5. Predictable fulfillment.
6. Low-friction defect and RMA resolution.
7. Fast repeat ordering for active repair-shop accounts.

The operating model is numbers-led. A single primary commercial metric and its
guardrails still need approval so agents cannot optimize revenue while harming
margin, cash flow, accuracy, or customer retention.

## Unresolved business facts

- Legal entity, conflict-resolution rights, and day-to-day operating roles.
- Current customer segmentation and which records are active repair shops.
- Premium versus A Grade definitions and evidence.
- Cost, margin, price-tier, minimum-order, and discount rules.
- Warehouse ownership, inventory procedure, and replenishment workflow.
- Current shipping cutoff, free-shipping threshold, carrier choices, and service
  levels.
- Warranty, defect evidence, RMA window, credits, replacement, and return
  freight rules.
- Accounting, CRM, email, support, and reporting systems of record.

No unresolved item should be turned into a public claim or automated write.
