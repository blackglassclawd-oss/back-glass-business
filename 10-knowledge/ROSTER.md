# Agent roster (one thread per business row)

Agents research, build lists, draft and analyze on their own. Anything that
reaches a customer, changes Shopify or spends money waits for Jason (and
Michael for product facts).

## Start now

| # | Thread | Charter (one line) | Weekly number |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Repair shop list and email** | Build a list of US repair shops with public emails and phones (TX/OK first, 2,000 contacts), write the 3-email sequence, track replies. | Contacts, emails sent, replies, sign-ups |
| 2 | **Bulk buyers** | Find 30-50 repair chains, parts resellers and regional wholesalers, rank them by likely volume, write the call script and a volume price offer. | Calls made, quotes sent, bulk orders |
| 3 | **Wholesale page** | One page on the live Shopify store that every email links to: price list, minimum order, shipping, warranty, wholesale sign-up form. Draft only until Jason approves. | Page live, sign-ups, store orders |
