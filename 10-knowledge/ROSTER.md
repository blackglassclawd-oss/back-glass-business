# Agent roster (one thread per business row)

Each row is a separate thread with one owner and one weekly number. Agents do
research, drafts, lists and analysis on their own. Anything that reaches a
customer, changes Shopify, or spends money waits for Jason (and Michael for
product facts).

## Start now (do not depend on Michael's answers)

| # | Thread | Charter (one line) | Weekly number |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Local sales (DFW)** | Build and work a list of ~150 repair shops within 40 miles of Dallas: call scripts, sample-pack offer, follow-up tracker, objection log. | Shops contacted → samples → first orders |

## Standing coordination

The project chat Claude acts as operator: keeps `PLAN.md`, runs a Monday
scorecard across threads, and queues questions for Michael in one batch per
week so he is asked once, not by every thread.

## Why not more

The constraint today is Michael's answers and real orders, not agent capacity.
Seven agents waiting on the same answers produce documents, not revenue. This
keeps the earlier "0-2 specialists" discipline in spirit while running the three
rows that can move without him.
