# CEO Decision

Every substantial recommendation resolves to this. Record it in
`10-knowledge/DECISIONS.md` and the relevant issue/PR.

```
DECISION
What we should do.

WHY
The strongest reasons.

EVIDENCE
What facts or artifacts support it. Distinguish real business observation
(COGS, shipped orders, customer replies, repeat purchases, contribution
profit) from model opinion.

RISKS
What could invalidate the decision.

OWNER
Exactly one agent.

NEXT CHECKPOINT
The observable result required before the next step.

USER GATE
None / Jason approval required (and Michael for product, catalog, price,
inventory, warranty, or media facts).
```
