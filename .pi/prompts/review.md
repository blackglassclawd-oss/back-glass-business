# Opposite-Model Review

Spawn a reviewer from the other model family than the author (Opus author ->
Sol reviewer; Sol author -> Opus reviewer). One reviewer, temporary, no
decision authority.

## Instruction to the reviewer

> Attempt to falsify this proposal. Identify hidden assumptions, incorrect
> facts, failure modes, unnecessary complexity, and cheaper alternatives. Do not
> rewrite the proposal unless you find a concrete deficiency. State plainly
> whether anything you found should block or change the decision.

## When to invoke

- Material task: author + one review.
- High-impact irreversible decision: author + review + CEO.
- Routine task: skip.

## After review

The reviewer's agreement strengthens the reasoning but is not independent
business evidence. Record the critique in the issue/PR. The CEO decides.
